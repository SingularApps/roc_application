The pilot is complete across iPhone, iPad and web. Payment runs through a payment-sheet fallback until tap-to-pay is available.

  • Offers, acceptance, results and the complete athlete list
  • Pre-banquet screen, CTA on Home, athlete list download
  • Stripe payment with the pending state synced to iPads
  • A test payment was confirmed in the CRM
Integrated — Nearly Complete
PCR

Web and mobile are integrated. This week’s adjustments are focused on the Kona tests.

  • Discharge diagnosis, vitals progression and share flow on web and mobile
  • Web: create a record without an athlete, T1/T2 location, location filter
  • Screenshots blocked on PCR screens
  • Kona medical team provisioned with the EMS role

  • Race and brand names no longer truncated in the race directory
  • Feedback modal no longer covers the event cards
  • Staff no longer duplicated in the Team & Operation drawer and header count
