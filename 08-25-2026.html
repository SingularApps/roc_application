OEA-18 · OEA-177 Spike Wrapping Up
Kiosk

Athlete-facing self-service for event registration and check-in, ending in bib and race-kit pickup. QR-code driven from the ticket email, iPad as the primary device. The exploration spike surfaced the biggest UX opportunity: team & relay management is currently so cumbersome that staff cancel and recreate the whole relay order just to swap a captain — that workaround should not survive into the new design.

Use Cases
Pre-registered check-in, walk-up registration, future-event sign-up
Roles
Volunteers handle standard check-in; staff handle changes, cancellations, relay edits
Improvement Areas
Team/relay management, modern payments incl. tap-to-pay, cancellation flow
Screens Going Into Design
  • Kiosk configuration & category setup
  • Athlete check-in (QR, manual fallback, waiver, ID check)
  • Ticket type change
  • Walk-up registration
  • Relay & team management

Testing & Rollout

● Phase 1 ran Tuesday, Aug 18 — next session likely Tuesday, Sep 1.

Phase 1 ran Tuesday, Aug 18 — a big thank you to everyone who joined and tested with us. We're planning another session with the same group, likely Tuesday, September 1. We'll confirm by Thursday (Aug 27), once we've run some additional internal tests. Detailed instructions will go out by email once it's locked in.

01
Approved Plan — 4 Phases

Crawl-walk-run, each gated by a feedback round and a go/no-go checkpoint before moving on.

Phase 1
Hybrid tabletop + guided tasks, remote, 1 hr — core group only
Gate: no critical bugs · confidence score · improvements non-blocking
Phase 2
Same format, wider team
Gate: go/no-go + feedback round
Phase 3
Field test in shadow/parallel mode, real race, no ops impact
Gate: go/no-go + feedback round
Phase 4
Live pilot, real event, app in production
02
Phase 1 Session Design (Locked)
Session Timebox — 1 Hour, Remote
0–10 min Access & Setup
10–45 min Guided Tasks
45–60 min Live Feedback
Tooling
  • Teams for facilitation
  • LogRocket for session recording & analysis
  • FigJam board for live feedback capture
Web + App In Parallel
  • Command center / leadership primarily on web; field staff on the app
  • Suggested: main tasks on web, app for notifications & chat
Features In Scope
  • General flow: login, profile, home dashboard, race/event page, chat, and live communications
  • Incident creation (basic)
  • Time-permitting: a light pass on the rest of incident management
Deferred To Focused Sessions
  • With the responsible ops teams: PCR, kiosk, slot allocations
Advance Criteria — Phase 1 → Phase 2
  • No critical bugs blocking features
  • Sufficient operational confidence score from participants
  • All improvement needs mapped and confirmed non-blocking

FigJam feedback board — one lane per participant, live checklist and colour-coded stickies during the session.
